# Restless Curiosity: Welcome to my Blog!

Hey, thank you for visiting my blog.

**I'm Francesco, a Senior Frontend Engineer originally from Sicily.**

Professionally, I started my journey at SOFTLAB S.p.A., followed by a remote role as a Frontend Engineer at CHILI Tech. This experience taught me the significance of being a **Product Contributor** rather than solely an engineer, highlighting the value of teamwork and collaborative success. 

Following CHILI Tech's acquisition by Globant, I continued my involvement in major streaming platforms like **[CHILI](https://chili.com/)** and **[FIFA+](https://plus.fifa.com)**, where I work on creating scalable and engaging user experiences.

**In 2024, after spending 7 years living in Rome, I decided it was time for a change.** 

I packed up and set off to explore the Canary Islands, spending several months between islands and embracing a more flexible lifestyle. 
It was during my time in **Fuerteventura**, alongside two friends, that the idea for [ISAAC](https://getisaac.it) was born—a startup focused on helping online businesses improve their Meta Ads performance and lead generation through a powerful SaaS product.

## Purpose of This Blog

I created this blog driven by my passion for writing and sharing knowledge, opinions or thoughts. 
Here, I'll explore a broad range of topics—from technology and software development to financial insights and reflections on life. This platform aims to offer thoughtful content, inspire meaningful discussions, and foster continuous learning.

  - The challenges and rewards of working in a remote-first environment
  - Tips for navigating the tech job market
- **Freelancing and Consulting tips & tricks**
